# Proposed /sell prices — awaiting owner review

These are **suggestions**, not active configuration or approved economy rules. They are separate from every player's /shop asking price. This first sample establishes the intended scale; it is not yet a price list for every Minecraft item.

Every amount below is for ONE item. A stack pays unit price × quantity. The maximum unit price is $10,000, so 64 items priced at $10,000 each would pay $640,000. Item Price starts OFF. Turning the tooltip OFF never disables selling.

## Decisions needed

1. Is this price scale close to what you want, or should the suggested values change? After you choose the scale, the complete per-material table still needs preparing and review.
2. Should named, enchanted, damaged and filled items sell for their base material price, with no extra value for contents/enchantments, or stay unsellable until special pricing is chosen? The existing development build keeps them unsellable and returns them. No new eligibility policy has been activated.

Items without an approved price also remain unsellable and return on closing /sell. Their tooltip explicitly says `Not sellable in /sell`, rather than displaying a made-up price. This is an unfinished price table, not the final requested every-item economy.
